how to cook friedrice

  1. take a bowl
  2. add 1/2 kg of rice
  3. add 1/2 kg of vegetables
  4. add 1/2 kg of spices
  5. mix all the ingredients
  6. cook it for 10 minutes
  7. serve it hot
